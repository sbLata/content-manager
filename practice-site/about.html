About Leafline

Leafline started as a small notebook of care tips shared between neighbours. Today we help thousands of home gardeners keep their plants healthy with practical guides and a monthly care subscription.

Leafline was founded in 2015 by two friends who kept killing their ferns and decided to learn why.
