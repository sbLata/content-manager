Welcome

Houseplants have a long history. The Victorians built glass cases called Wardian cases to carry ferns across oceans, and by the late nineteenth century no fashionable parlour was complete without a palm or an aspidistra. Interest faded during the twentieth century, then returned strongly in the 1970s, when spider plants and macrame hangers filled apartments everywhere. Today's revival is driven by small homes, remote work and social media.
