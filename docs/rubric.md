# Rubric: user-friendliness and content positioning (draft)

Each page is scored 1 to 5 on every criterion. A page is flagged when any criterion scores 2 or lower.
1 = clearly fails, 3 = acceptable, 5 = excellent.

## User-friendliness

| Criterion | Score 1 looks like | Score 5 looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scannable | One long block of text, no headings | Clear headings, paragraphs of 4 sentences or fewer |
| Plain language | Unexplained jargon for the page's audience | Words the intended reader already uses |
| Clear steps | Instructions buried in one sentence | Numbered steps, one action each |
| Clear next action | Reader can't tell what to do next | One obvious next step (link, button, or instruction) |

## Content positioning

| Criterion | Score 1 looks like | Score 5 looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audience match | Language and depth wrong for the stated audience | Language and depth fit the stated audience |
| Headline clarity | Headline says nothing about the page's value | Headline states what the reader gets |
| Answers the visit | Page mostly covers something other than its purpose | Page delivers what its title promises |
| Fits the site goal | Page doesn't support the site's goal | Page moves the reader toward the site's goal |

## Site context used for judging

- Site: Leafline, a plant-care blog and subscription service.
- Audience: home gardeners, from complete beginners to experienced growers. Pages that state their audience are judged against it.
- Site goal: help readers care for plants, and convert them to a monthly care plan.

## How to validate this rubric

Two people score every practice page independently. Any criterion where scores differ by 2 or more is reworded and rescored.
