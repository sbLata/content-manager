Integrated pest management for experienced growers

This guide is for experienced growers who already know basic plant care and want a systematic approach to pests.

Principles

Integrated pest management (IPM) combines monitoring, prevention and targeted treatment. Start with the least disruptive control that works.

Monitoring

Check the undersides of leaves weekly. Yellow sticky traps help you track fungus gnat and thrips populations over time.

Identifying spider mites

Identify spider mites by the fine webbing and stippling pattern shown in the photo below. Compare your leaves with it before choosing a treatment.

Treatment options

Begin with physical controls such as wiping leaves and isolating affected plants. Move to horticultural oils or insecticidal soap if populations grow. Rotate products to avoid resistance.

Biological controls

Predatory mites such as Phytoseiulus persimilis are effective against spider mites in warm, humid conditions.

Prevention

Quarantine new plants for two weeks and keep humidity steady.
